Poured slowly, cured properly

Every candle is made by hand in a small batch, then left to rest before it ships.

  1. Melt

    Soy and coconut wax is melted and held at 75 °C so it stays smooth.

  2. Scent

    Fragrance oil is stirred in at 65 °C. We use 9% oil: enough to fill a room without a harsh edge.

  3. Pour

    Each jar is poured in two stages to keep the top flat.

  4. Cure

    Candles rest for ten days so the scent settles into the wax.

Burn guide

Three habits that give you every one of the 55 hours. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

How long should the first burn last?

Until the wax melts to the edge of the jar, usually 2 to 3 hours. This prevents tunnelling and keeps the burn even.

How short should I trim the wick?

To about 5 mm before each burn. A short wick gives a steady flame and less soot.

How do I put the candle out?

Use a snuffer, or dip the wick into the wax and lift it back up. Blowing it out sends smoke into the room.
